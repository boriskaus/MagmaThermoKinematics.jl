# Authors

[MagmaThermoKinematics.jl](https://github.com/boriskaus/MagmaThermoKinematics.jl)'s development is coordinated by a group of *principal developers*,
who are also its main contributors and who can be contacted in case of
questions about [MagmaThermoKinematics.jl](https://github.com/boriskaus/MagmaThermoKinematics.jl). Together, these two groups form "The MagmaThermoKinematics.jl Authors".
