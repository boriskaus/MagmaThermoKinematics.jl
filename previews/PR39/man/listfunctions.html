MagmaThermoKinematics.EruptionParams Type
julia
EruptionParams(; kwargs...)

Parameters of the chamber/eruption model. Pressures in Pa, lengths in m, times in s.

  • ϕ_erupt: minimum melt fraction of a mobile chamber cell

  • h_melt_min: minimum melt thickness (∫ϕ dz equivalent) for a region to count as a chamber [m]

  • ΔP_crit, ΔP_relax: overpressure at which the chamber drains, and the one it relaxes to

  • ϕ_g_crit: gas volume fraction above which the chamber is gas-locked and cannot erupt

  • σ_barrier: stress barrier that the dike has to overcome [Pa]

  • w_dike, κ_magma: dike half-width [m] and magma thermal diffusivity [m²/s] (dike freezing)

  • μ_shear: host-rock shear modulus [Pa]; ν: Poisson ratio used for the displacement field

  • A_visc, G_act: Arrhenius constants of the crustal creep law

  • m_w: total H₂O mass fraction of the magma

  • ρ_melt, ρ_x, crust: densities as (ρ0, α, β) (reference density, expansivity, compressibility)

  • melt_viscosity, solubility, ρ_gas: GeoParams laws

  • g: gravity

MagmaThermoKinematics.AdvectTemperature! Method
julia
AdvectTemperature!(Tnew, T, Grid, Velocity, dt)

Semi-Lagrangian advection of T by the displacement field Velocity (one array per direction, in meters, defined on the grid nodes) over pseudo-time dt, into Tnew. Grid is the tuple of 1D coordinate ranges. The spacing is constant, so every departure point is index arithmetic; the RK2 velocity sample and the temperature read are multilinear. This is the RK2/linear scheme of AdvectTemperature, as a kernel on the backend of T.

Tnew must not alias T: the departure point of one node generally reads values other nodes still need.

MagmaThermoKinematics.MTK_GeoParams Method
julia
Grid, Arrays, Tracers, Dikes, time_props = MTK_GeoParams(Mat_tup::Tuple, Num::NumericalParameters, Dikes::SillParameters; CartData_input=nothing, time_props::TimeDependentProperties = TimeDepProps());

Main routine that performs a 2D, 2D axisymmetric or 3D thermal diffusion simulation with injection of dikes. The model is 3D if Num.Ny > 0 (or if CartData_input is 3D), and 2D otherwise. The model arrays live on the KernelAbstractions backend Num.backend, with element type Num.FloatType.

Parameters

  • Mat_tup::Tuple: Tuple of material properties.

  • Num::NumericalParameters: Numerical parameters.

  • Dikes::SillParameters: Intrusion parameters.

  • CartData_input::CartData: Optional input of a CartData structure generated with GeophysicalModelGenerator.

  • time_props::TimeDependentProperties: Optional input of a TimeDependentProperties structure.

  • callbacks::NamedTuple: Optional per-simulation replacements of the customizable functions below, e.g. callbacks = (; print_output = my_print, update_TimeDepProps! = my_props!); the keys are the function names without the MTK_ prefix and the arguments are the same. Callbacks only affect this simulation, so several simulations with different callbacks can run in one session, also concurrently.

Customizable functions

There are a few functions that you can overwrite in your user code (or replace for a single simulation with callbacks) to customize the simulation:

  • MTK_visualize_output(Grid::GridData, Num::NumericalParameters, Arrays::NamedTuple, Mat_tup::Tuple, Dikes::SillParameters)

  • MTK_update_TimeDepProps!(time_props::TimeDependentProperties, Grid::GridData, Num::NumericalParameters, Arrays::NamedTuple, Mat_tup::Tuple, Dikes::SillParameters)

  • MTK_update_ArraysStructs!(Arrays::NamedTuple, Grid::GridData, Dikes::SillParameters, Num::NumericalParameters, Mat_tup::Tuple)

  • MTK_initialize!(Arrays::NamedTuple, Grid::GridData, Num::NumericalParameters, Tracers::StructArray, Dikes::SillParameters, CartData_input)

  • MTK_updateTracers(Grid::GridData, Arrays::NamedTuple, Tracers::StructArray, Dikes::SillParameters, time_props::TimeDependentProperties, Num::NumericalParameters)

  • MTK_save_output(Grid::GridData, Arrays::NamedTuple, Tracers::StructArray, Dikes::SillParameters, time_props::TimeDependentProperties, Num::NumericalParameters, CartData_input::CartData)

  • MTK_inject_dikes(Grid::GridData, Num::NumericalParameters, Arrays::NamedTuple, Mat_tup::Tuple, Dikes::SillParameters, Tracers::StructVector)

  • MTK_source!(Arrays::NamedTuple, Grid::GridData, Num::NumericalParameters, Mat_tup::Tuple, Dikes::SillParameters)

  • MTK_initialize!(Arrays::NamedTuple, Grid::GridData, Num::NumericalParameters, Tracers::StructArray, Dikes::SillParameters)

  • MTK_finalize!(Arrays::NamedTuple, Grid::GridData, Num::NumericalParameters, Tracers::StructArray, Dikes::SillParameters, CartData_input::CartData)

Geosyst. 12, https://doi.org/10.1029/2011GC00356

  • BackgroundPhase: The background phase (used for places that don't have cells, nor surrounding cells )

  • ReturnNumTracers: Return the number of tracers on every grid cell (default=false)

MagmaThermoKinematics.apt_diffusion_solve! Method
julia
apt_diffusion_solve!(Arrays, dt, Δ, R, Num)

Solve one implicit diffusion step for the frozen properties in Arrays (Kc, Rho, Cp, Hl, dϕdT, Hr) starting from the guess Arrays.T_it_old (the properties are those at this iterate, with melt fraction Arrays.ϕ), with Arrays.T and Arrays.pt_phiold the temperature and melt fraction of the previous time level, and store the result in Arrays.Tnew. Boundary conditions are those of the explicit step: zero flux on the lateral faces, isothermal (T of the previous time level) or a prescribed flux on the bottom.

Returns the number of pseudo-transient iterations used. Throws an error if Num.apt_tol (relative to max(|T|, 1)) is not reached within Num.apt_max_iter iterations.

julia
chamber_step!(state, ep, ϕ, T, coords; recharge, Δt, time, T_background, margin=0.0) -> event

Advance the chamber of the arrays ϕ (melt fraction) and T [°C] (host or device) by Δt [s]. coords are the 1D coordinate vectors (z last, negative downward, free surface at z = 0), recharge the magma volume injected into the chamber per second in native units (m²/s in 2D, m³/s in 3D) and T_background(depth) the undisturbed geotherm [°C] at depth [m].

MagmaThermoKinematics.inject_sills Method
julia
Tracers, Tnew, InjectedVolume, dike_poly, Velocity =
    inject_sills(Tracers, T, Grid, sill, T_in, Phase_in, nTr_dike;
                 AdvectionMethod="RK2", InterpolationMethod="Linear",
                 dike_poly=[])

Inject a sill/dike described by the InjectSills.AbstractSill object sill into the temperature field T defined on the regular grid Grid.

Arguments

  • Tracers – StructArray of Tracer objects (may be unassigned on first call)

  • T – temperature array [°C], mutated in-place; any backend

  • Grid – 1-D coordinate vectors (x, z) in 2-D or (x, y, z) in 3-D

  • sill – AbstractSill (e.g. PennyShapedSill) with the desired center, orientation, size, and elastic parameters already set

  • T_in – temperature of the injected magma [°C]

  • Phase_in – rock-phase index assigned to new tracers

  • nTr_dike – number of new tracers to seed inside the sill

Keyword arguments

  • AdvectionMethod – "RK2" (default) or "Euler"

  • InterpolationMethod – "Linear", "Quadratic", or "Cubic" (default "Linear")

  • dike_poly – optional plotting polygon (InjectSills.dike_polygon) that is advected with the host rock

Returns

(Tracers, Tnew, InjectedVolume, dike_poly, Velocity), where InjectedVolume is the equivalent 3D volume of sill in m³ (InjectSills.volume).

Algorithm

The temperature field is advected by the displacement field of InjectSills.hostrock_displacement! over nsteps pseudo-time steps, so that the displacement per step stays below 0.5 * min(dx, dz). With the default RK2/linear scheme this runs on the backend of T; the other schemes need a CPU Array. Existing tracers and dike_poly move from x to x + u(x), with u evaluated at their own positions. add_dike then sets T = T_in inside the sill and seeds the new tracers.

The displacement field (= velocity for pseudo-time dt_total = 1) is obtained directly from the sill object, which already encodes the center and orientation of the intrusion — no external rotation is needed.

MagmaThermoKinematics.mogi_displacement! Method
julia
mogi_displacement!(U, X, centers, ΔV; L=zeros, ν=0.25, softening)

Displacement U (tuple of arrays, one per direction) at the node coordinates X (tuple of arrays) due to Mogi point sources at centers (Ns × N) with volume changes ΔV (Ns; positive = inflation, negative = withdrawal). Each source contributes (1−ν) ΔV/π · d/|d|³, which is the InjectSills.MogiSphere solution written in terms of its volume change ΔV = π ΔP r³/G; the contributions are summed (linear elasticity). In a 2D section a source with L[s] > 0 is instead a line of point sources along the out-of-plane direction over [-L, L], with ΔV the volume change per unit length: the integrated kernel is 2L · d/(|d|² √(|d|² + L²)). The sum runs on the backend of X[1]; softening [m] regularises the singularity at the sources' own nodes. With halfspace = :image the sources are volume-consistent dilatation centres (ΔV/(4π) · d/|d|³, whose divergence integrates to ΔV) with opposite-sign images mirrored at z_surface (surface uplift twice the full-space value); halfspace = :mindlin (3-D point sources) is the exact half-space solution of Mindlin & Cheng (1950) — volume change ΔV in the bulk and the Mogi displacement at the free surface z_surface; the :mogi kernel is the surface formula of the half-space solution and over-dilates the interior by 4(1 − ν).

MagmaThermoKinematics.move_free_surface! Method
julia
move_free_surface!(Phases, Phases_init, T, Zsurf, coords, U; air=0, fill=1) -> Zsurf

Move the free surface (the boundary between the air phase and the rock below it) with the displacement field U [m] (tuple of arrays, vertical component last). Zsurf holds the surface elevation of every vertical column (size of the horizontal grid; NaN entries are initialised from the phases, half a cell above the highest rock cell), and is shifted by the vertical displacement at the surface, so displacements smaller than a cell accumulate. Cells above the surface become air, cells below it that were air become fill; Phases_init is updated the same way so that the initial-phase reset after injections keeps the moved surface, and T of cells turned into air takes the air temperature above them. All arrays may live on any backend.
