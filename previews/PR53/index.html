What Is MagmaThermoKinematics.jl? ​

MagmaThermoKinematics.jl is a Julia package for simulating the thermal evolution of magmatic systems in 2D, 2D axisymmetric and 3D. It supports intrusive events such as dike and sill emplacement, tracks tracers, and couples thermal transport with material properties and phase/melting effects.

Beyond the thermal evolution, it can follow the magma chamber that forms from the intrusions:

  • Overpressure and eruptions: the overpressure of the chamber follows from the mass balance of a compressible, water-bearing magma and relaxes viscously into the host rock (with a shell viscosity from the temperature field and GeoParams creep laws). The chamber erupts once the overpressure is high enough for a dike to reach the surface; the erupted volume is removed, and the host rock and the free surface move accordingly (Eruptions).

  • Convective cooling: convection in melt-rich regions is parameterised by an effective conductivity from a Nusselt–Rayleigh relation, and heat lost out of the plane of a 2D section can be included (Numerics and Physics).

  • Zircon ages: tracers record the temperature–time paths of the emplaced magma, from which zircon age distributions are computed (Zircon ages).

The package uses finite-difference discretizations, explicit or implicit (Implicit Enthalpy Solver) time stepping on uniform or refined grids (Grid Refinement), and runs on CPUs and GPUs.
