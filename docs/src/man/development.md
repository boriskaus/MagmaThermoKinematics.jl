# Ongoing Development

MagmaThermoKinematics.jl is part of broader development toward more general magmatic systems software in the MAGMA project.

Current and planned directions include:

- Thermal diffusion solvers with kinematic dike emplacement.
- Extension toward mechanical multiphysics solvers (for example stress and deformation-rate evolution).
- Modular and reusable package-based workflows across JuliaGeodynamics tools.

A key design goal is reusability of physical parameterizations across packages. For example, GeoParams.jl definitions of density, heat capacity, and conductivity can be reused by multiple simulation codes.

For broader ecosystem development, see the [JuliaGeodynamics](https://github.com/JuliaGeodynamics/) or [PTSolvers](https://github.com/PTSolvers/) organization.
